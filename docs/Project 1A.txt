| Regime              | One-word meaning | Simple explanation                                               |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Risk-On**         | **Confident**    | Investors are optimistic and willing to buy risky assets.        |
| **Risk-Off**        | **Fearful**      | Investors are scared and prefer safer assets or cash.            |
| **High Volatility** | **Chaotic**      | Prices move up and down very quickly.                            |
| **Low Volatility**  | **Calm**         | Prices move slowly with small fluctuations.                      |
| **Trending**        | **Directional**  | The market is consistently moving in one direction (up or down). |
| **Sideways**        | **Stagnant**     | The market isn't moving much; it stays within a range.           |

Phase 2 flow
2.1 Research Strategy
Define exactly what we need to learn and how we'll evaluate research.
2.2 Market Regime Detection Research
Understand how financial regimes are defined and detected.
2.3 HMM / Bayesian HMM
Study the core regime-switching approach.
2.4 Regime-Switching VAR
Understand multivariate regime dynamics.
2.5 Bayesian Neural Networks
Study nonlinear modelling and uncertainty.
2.6 Time-Series Foundation Models
Evaluate models such as Chronos/TimesFM and whether they actually add value here.
2.7 Sequential Inference
Study particle filtering and online state estimation.
2.8 Changepoint Detection
Study BOCPD and its role in detecting regime transitions.
2.9 Ensemble Methods
Study Bayesian Model Averaging and time-aware stacking.
2.10 Uncertainty & Calibration
Study epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty and conformal prediction.
2.11 Financial Features & Economic Rationale
Determine which market, flow and macro variables make economic sense.
2.12 Method Comparison Matrix
Compare all candidate approaches on accuracy, uncertainty, interpretability, complexity, data requirements and suitability.
2.13 Research Conclusions
Decide what we will use, test, reject, or keep open.
